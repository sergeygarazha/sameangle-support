Before & After photo comparisons for detailing

SameAngle helps you show the difference your work makes: capture Before, repeat the same angle with a translucent reference, cover private details and share a clean JPEG or 6-second MP4 comparison.

Answers

Why does the app ask for camera access?

Only when you tap Capture — the camera records the Before/After photos. If you decline, you can still use Import from Photos, and you can change permission anytime in iOS Settings. A translucent reference overlay and an optional grid help you repeat the same angle for the After shot.

Are the overlay and grid saved into my photos?

No. They exist only on the capture screen. The saved photo is the live camera frame alone — the reference image, transparency slider and grid are never written into pixels.

Where are my projects stored?

Entirely on your device — no account, no server. Deleting a project deletes its photos and edits; exports you have already shared are separate copies. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Do exported files contain location or camera data?

No. JPEG and MP4 exports contain only the rendered comparison — no GPS, no original EXIF, no camera serial number or dates.

What does SameAngle Pro include?

SameAngle Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks MP4 comparisons and unlimited JPEG exports (1:1 and 9:16). The built-in sample project exports for free, and your first successful JPEG export is free. Projects, photos, alignment and cover areas are always available to view and edit without a purchase.

How do I restore a purchase or get a refund?

Open Pro → Restore Purchases in the app and sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why is the sample project labeled "edited/generated"?

The built-in sample uses an illustrative interior pair so you can try the editor and exports without your own photos. It is clearly marked as an illustration, not a real cleaning result. Replacing its photos with your own turns it into a regular project.
