Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

SameAngle creates Before/After photo comparisons on your device. It does not require an account and has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no developer-operated server. We do not receive your photos or project files.

Photos and camera

Camera access is requested only when you choose Capture. You can decline and import photos instead, or change camera access later in Settings. Import uses Apple's photo picker and gives SameAngle access only to the items you select.

Photos, project names, alignment, cover areas and other edits are stored in the app's local container on your device. Your device backup settings decide whether this data is included in backups. Deleting a project removes its working photos and edits. Exported files and copies you have already saved or shared are separate and may remain outside that project.

Cover rectangles hide the regions you select in a rendered comparison; your original photos stay available in the project for editing. JPEG and MP4 exports do not include the original photo's GPS, camera serial number or other source metadata.

Sharing

An exported file leaves the app only when you choose a destination in the system Share menu. The privacy practices of that recipient or service apply to that copy.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for SameAngle Pro. SameAngle uses StoreKit to verify purchase rights and supports Restore Purchases. We never receive your payment card information. The app stores locally whether the free JPEG export has been used. Viewing and editing projects never require a purchase.

Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
